# Video Script: Harvia Q4 2024 Earnings Summary

**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Format:** Earnings summary video (prototype for IR teams)  
**Tone:** Professional, energetic, data-driven  
**Audience:** Retail investors, analysts, stakeholders  

---

## Script

**[0:00-0:08] HOOK + HEADLINE**

> "Harvia just posted their best quarter ever. Here's what you need to know about Q4 2024."

*Visual: Harvia logo → Q4 2024 text → Record breaking graphic*

---

**[0:08-0:20] REVENUE STORY**

> "Revenue hit 51 million euros—up 29% year-over-year. That's an all-time quarterly record. Organic growth alone was 22%."

*Visual: Revenue bar chart showing EUR 39.4M → EUR 51.0M with +29.3% arrow*

---

**[0:20-0:35] GEOGRAPHIC BREAKDOWN**

> "The growth driver? North America. The region is now Harvia's largest market, fueled by exploding interest in saunas for health and wellness. They crushed it on Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Asia-Pacific also delivered strong growth with big project wins."

*Visual: World map highlighting North America (bright), APAC (bright), Europe (dimmer). Quick cut to sauna product shots.*

---

**[0:35-0:45] PROFITABILITY TRADE-OFF**

> "Profitability took a hit—adjusted EBIT margin dropped to 17%, below their 20% target. Why? Lower-margin campaign sales and big investments in growth initiatives. They're playing the long game."

*Visual: Margin chart showing 24.2% → 17.1% with downward arrow. Text: "Growth investments"*

---

**[0:45-0:52] FULL YEAR PERFORMANCE**

> "For the full year, Harvia delivered: 16% revenue growth, 21% adjusted EBIT margin—meeting their long-term target—and all while integrating their ThermaSol acquisition."

*Visual: Three checkmarks next to: ✓ 16% growth, ✓ 21% margin, ✓ ThermaSol*

---

**[0:52-0:60] FORWARD LOOK + CTA**

> "2025 marks Harvia's 75th anniversary. They're entering it from a position of strength—with North America and wellness trends at their back. Premium sauna is having a moment."

*Visual: "75 years" badge → Harvia products → upward trending graph → End card: "Harvia Plc | Q4 2024"*

## Voiceover Notes

**Tone:** Confident, upbeat, but honest about trade-offs  
**Pacing:** Fast but clear (60 seconds is tight)  
**Emphasis:** "All-time record" / "29% growth" / "North America" / "75th anniversary"  

**Style references:**
- Think "Bloomberg Quicktake" speed
- Mix of data + narrative (not just numbers)
- Acknowledge the margin compression honestly (builds trust)

## Production Options

### Option A: Manual Editing (High Quality)
- Record voiceover (professional or AI voice like ElevenLabs)
- Edit in DaVinci Resolve / Final Cut / Premiere
- Create graphics in Figma / Canva → export PNGs
- Source product images from Harvia website/press kit
- Add background music (upbeat, corporate)
- **Time:** 2-3 hours for first video
- **Cost:** $0-50 (stock music, AI voice)

### Option B: Remotion (Programmatic, Scalable)
- Write React components for each scene
- Pull data from JSON (harvia_q4_2024_metrics.json)
- Generate video programmatically
- Easy to template for future earnings
- **Time:** 4-6 hours initial setup, 30 min per video after
- **Cost:** Remotion license + rendering credits

### Recommendation: Start with Option A
- Faster to produce first prototype
- Show IR teams what's possible
- Gather feedback before investing in automation
