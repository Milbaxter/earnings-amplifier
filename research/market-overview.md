# Earnings Amplifier - Market Research

## Market Opportunity

### Target Segment
**Nordic small/mid-cap public companies with under-resourced IR teams**
- Market cap: €50M - €1B
- Industries: "Boring" sectors (logistics, manufacturing, industrials, utilities, food)
- Listed on Nasdaq Helsinki, Stockholm, Copenhagen exchanges
- Pain point: Complex financials, hard to communicate value to retail investors and media

### Market Size

**Nasdaq Helsinki:**
- ~190 listed companies total
- ~50-80 small/mid-cap (€50M-€1B market cap) in target sectors
- Estimated IR budget per company: €50K-200K/year

**Nasdaq Stockholm:**
- ~400+ listed companies
- ~100-150 in target segment

**Nasdaq Copenhagen:**
- ~150+ listed companies
- ~50-80 in target segment

**Total Addressable Market (Nordic):**
- ~200-300 target companies across all three exchanges
- Avg IR budget: €100K/year
- TAM: ~€20-30M/year in IR spending

### Competitive Landscape

#### Traditional IR firms:
- **Brunswick Group, FTI Consulting, Morrow Sodali:** High-touch, strategic advisory (€100K+/year)
- **Finsbury Glover Hering:** Communications/PR focus, expensive
- **Local Nordic IR agencies:** Smaller, relationship-driven, not tech-enabled

**Gap:** No one offers automated, tech-enabled earnings content generation. Current options:
- Expensive strategic IR consultants (overkill for small caps)
- DIY in-house (time-consuming, inconsistent quality)
- Legacy IR platforms (distribution, not content creation)

#### Adjacent tech players:
- **Q4 Inc, Workiva, Notified:** Investor relations platforms (websites, webcasts, compliance)
- **Focus:** Distribution + compliance, NOT content creation
- **No one doing:** Auto-generate dashboards/videos/blogs from earnings data

### Value Proposition

**What we solve:**
1. **Time:** IR teams spend days creating earnings materials → automate in hours
2. **Reach:** Traditional PDFs don't reach retail investors → create shareable content
3. **Storytelling:** Data-heavy financials bore investors → visualize & simplify
4. **Media:** Hard to get press coverage → create media-ready content packages

**Deliverables (per quarter):**
- Interactive earnings dashboard (web-based, embeddable)
- 60-90 sec highlight video (CEO voiceover optional)
- Plain-English blog post with charts
- Social media package (Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, memes)
- Media pitch kit (journalist-friendly summaries)

### Pricing Strategy

#### Tier 1: Self-Service (€2K/quarter, €8K/year)
- Upload earnings PDF/transcript
- Auto-generate dashboard + blog + basic social content
- DIY distribution

#### Tier 2: Managed (€5K/quarter, €20K/year)
- Everything in Tier 1
- Custom video production (scriptwriting, voiceover, editing)
- Social media posting/scheduling
- Basic media outreach

#### Tier 3: Enterprise (€50-100K/year)
- Full IR automation (all 4 quarters)
- White-glove service (custom dashboards, branded content)
- Media relations (pitch to journalists, secure coverage)
- IR strategy consulting

### Customer Segments

**Primary: Small/mid-cap public companies (€50M-€500M)**
- IR team of 1-2 people (stretched thin)
- Limited budget vs. large caps
- Hungry for investor attention
- Target: 30-50 customers Year 1

**Secondary: IR agencies (white-label)**
- Buy Earnings Amplifier tech, rebrand for clients
- Subscription model: €10K/year + per-client fees

**Tertiary: Pre-IPO companies**
- Building IR muscle before going public

## Validation Plan

### Phase 1: Build Manual Prototype (Week 1-2)
- Pick 2 target companies (Posti, Harvia)
- Download Q4 2024 earnings reports
- Manually create full Earnings Amplifier output:
  - Dashboard (Observable or Streamlit)
  - Video (60 sec, Remotion or manual)
  - Blog post + social content

### Phase 2: Cold Outreach (Week 3)
- Email IR teams at 10 companies with prototype samples
- Subject: "Built this for your earnings — feedback?"
- Include live links (dashboard, video, blog)
- Ask: Would you use this? What would you pay?

### Phase 3: Iterate & Close Pilots (Week 4-6)
- Target: 3 pilot customers (free for Q1 2025, paid after)
- Deliver Q1 earnings content in exchange for feedback
- Refine product based on real use cases
- Convert to paid (€2-5K/quarter)

### Phase 4: Scale (Month 3-6)
- Productize (build automation scripts, reduce manual work)
- Expand outreach (50+ companies)
- Target: 10 paying customers by Month 6

## Key Risks

1. **Low willingness to pay:** IR teams may not value content enough to pay €20K/year
2. **"We'll build it ourselves":** Larger companies with dev resources might copy the idea
3. **Regulatory concerns:** Public companies cautious about auto-generated financial content
4. **Competitor response:** Existing IR platforms add content generation features
5. **Market education:** Companies don't yet know they need this (category creation problem)

## Mitigation Strategies

- Start with free pilots to prove ROI (increased investor engagement, media coverage)
- Add compliance layer (review/approve all content before publishing)
- Build IP around automation workflows (parsing, video generation)
- Move fast — launch before competitors notice
- Show case studies: "Company X got 5 media mentions after using Earnings Amplifier"

## Unique Insights

**Why this works in Nordics:**
- Nordic companies = high quality, low visibility
- Language barrier (Finnish/Swedish earnings → English content for global investors)
- Smaller IR budgets than US/UK → more price-sensitive, need efficiency
- "Boring" industries = harder to get media attention → bigger need for modern storytelling

**Why this works NOW:**
- Generative AI makes content automation feasible (GPT-4 for copy, Remotion for video)
- Retail investing boom (people want accessible financial content)
- ESG/transparency pressure (companies need to communicate more, not less)
- Remote/digital everything (dashboards > PDF downloads)
