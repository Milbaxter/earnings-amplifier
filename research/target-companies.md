# Earnings Amplifier - Target Companies

## Nordic Small/Mid-Cap Public Companies (Helsinki Stock Exchange)

### Selection Criteria
- Market cap: €50M - €1B (small/mid-cap)
- Industry: "Boring" sectors (logistics, manufacturing, industrials, utilities)
- Listed on Nasdaq Helsinki
- Regular quarterly earnings releases
- Likely to have under-resourced IR teams

## Validation Approach

### Phase 1: Build Manual Prototypes (Week 1-2)

Pick **2 companies** from Tier 1 (suggest: Posti + Harvia)

**For each:**
1. Download latest quarterly earnings report (PDF + transcript if available)
2. Extract key metrics (revenue, EBITDA, segments, YoY/QoQ trends)
3. Build manual dashboard (use Observable, Streamlit, or simple HTML/Tailwind)
4. Create 60-sec video summary (Remotion or manual editing)
5. Write plain-English blog post with charts
6. Draft Twitter thread + LinkedIn post

### Phase 2: Outreach to IR Teams (Week 3)

**Email template:**

---

**Subject:** Built this for your Q4 earnings — feedback?

Hi [IR Contact Name],

I built an automated earnings dashboard for [Company] as a proof of concept. Takes your quarterly data and turns it into:
- Interactive web dashboard (see link below)
- 60-second video summary
- Plain-English blog post
- Social media content

This was all auto-generated from your latest earnings PDF. Would love your feedback — is this something IR teams would actually use?

[Dashboard Link]  
[Video Link]

Happy to chat for 15 min if you're open to it.

Best,  
Mili

---

### Phase 3: Track Interest & Iterate (Week 4)

- How many IR teams respond?
- What features do they want?
- What would they pay?
- Objections/concerns?
